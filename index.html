Our story

Since 2018, Theeshvaan Traders has been bringing organic goodness to your plate. Our honey is naturally harvested from kombu, moringa, vaagai and wild forest flowers, and bottled raw, unfiltered and unprocessed.

Nothing is added and nothing is taken away, so each honey keeps the taste and colour of the flowers it comes from.
